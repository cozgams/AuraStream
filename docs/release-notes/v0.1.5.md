# AuraStream v0.1.5

> **This App Created by AAA**  
> Official release `v0.1.5` (`0.1.5` / Android versionCode `3000105`).

### What's New & Improvements in v0.1.5

1. **GitHub Update Checking Fixed**:
   - Fixed repository identifier in Rust backend (`src-tauri/src/api.rs`) from `AAA/AuraStream` to `cozgams/AuraStream`.
   - The in-app update checker (`api.checkUpdate()`) now correctly queries `https://api.github.com/repos/cozgams/AuraStream/releases/latest` to discover new releases and platform-specific assets.

2. **Official About & Repository Links**:
   - Updated the About modal in `src/pages/Settings.tsx` to point to the official `https://github.com/cozgams/AuraStream` repository.
   - Added persistent author signature `AuraStream Client v{APP_VERSION} · Created by AAA`.

3. **Audio Ducking & Video Volume Restoration**:
   - Fixed an audio ducking bug in `src/subtitles/voiceDubService.ts` where video volume was unconditionally reset to `1.0` (100%) after Text-to-Speech finished.
   - Now captures and remembers the user's pre-duck volume (`preDuckVolume`), accurately restoring muted states and custom volume preferences upon utterance completion or playback cancellation.

4. **Multiline Caption Formatting**:
   - Added `whiteSpace: "pre-wrap"` to `src/components/SubtitleOverlay.tsx` ensuring multiline dialogue cues preserve their intended line breaks without colliding or flowing off-screen.

5. **Multi-Platform CI/CD & Artifact Builds**:
   - Full automated builds and releases for Android (`arm64-v8a`, `armeabi-v7a`, `x86_64`), Windows (`NSIS` & portable `ZIP`), macOS (Universal `DMG`), Linux (`DEB`), and iOS (`IPA`).
